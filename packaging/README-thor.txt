FivePocket Thor Edition {version} (AYN Thor)
==========================================

This app contains NO game code and NO game data. You need the two Xbox 360 discs of the game that you own, and the
FivePocket Thor Edition Builder for Windows (FivePocket-Thor-Edition-Builder-{version}-windows-x64.zip, a
separate download of the same version).

NEEDS
-----
- An AYN Thor (the lower screen shows the map, the phone and touch controls).
- About 19 GB free for the game files and 0.5 GB for the built package.

STEPS
-----
1. Build your package with the FivePocket Thor Edition Builder from your own discs (see the builder's README.txt).
2. Install FivePocket-Thor-Edition-{version}.apk.
3. Open the app and follow its first-run screen: it imports the package and your game files into its own storage.

The app's About page shows app version 1.0.3-alpha-thor: that is this release's app ({version}); the next update
gives the app the release's version number.

Updating from a closed-alpha app: it was signed with another key, so it must be uninstalled first, and uninstalling
deletes its saves. Copy your saves out first.

NEVER SHARE WHAT YOU BUILD
--------------------------
Never share, upload or sell: disc images, files copied from your discs, the .bundle the builder makes, the
builder's work folder, the app's game files or caches, or your saves. They come from the game.
