# Security and takedown contact

- **Security problems** (a way to make the app or builder run code it should not, a leak of personal data): please
  do not open a public issue. Use **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's **Security** tab (a private report
  to the maintainer, [@marcm0de](https://github.com/marcm0de)) with the steps to reproduce.
- **Takedown and rights requests**, and reports of material that must not be here (game content, keys, personal
  data): use the same private report, or the public **Content report** form without attaching the material.
  Formal copyright notices can also go through GitHub's own
  [DMCA process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

We answer within 7 days, remove confirmed material promptly and say what was removed.

For everything else (questions, help, showing your play) use the FivePocket Discord: https://discord.gg/mZvqCsxgxT. Reports of material that
must not be here still go through the private report or the **Content report** form above, not Discord.
