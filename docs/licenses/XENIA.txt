Depth sample counter byte layout adapted from src/xenia/gpu/xenos.h,
xe_gpu_depth_sample_counts, in xenos_depth_sample_counts.h.
This adaptation is a read-only codec and wrapping-delta calculation, not the
fixed sample-count workaround in command_processor.cc. Query execution and
completion are not authorized by decoding the bytes.

Signed five-bit texture-offset conversion (half-texel units) follows
src/xenia/gpu/ucode.h, TextureFetchInstruction::offset_x/offset_y, Copyright 2022
Ben Vanik. Local: tools/rpf7_inventory/pixel_sample_dataflow.py. Explicit
profiles and all other fetch addressing/modifier guards remain unchanged.
Capture-only texture-slot selection in xenos_shader_texture_slots.h also
uses ucode.h's offset/LOD fields and shader_translator.cc's setTexLOD
has_dest=false classification. No texture sampling or draw admission follows.

Bounded geometry-LOD CPU reference arithmetic also follows ucode.h scalar
LOG_IEEE(16), RECIP_IEEE(19), EXP_IEEE(14) and the SM3 zero-multiply handling in
src/xenia/gpu/spirv_shader_translator_alu.cc. Local: pixel_sample_dataflow.py,
geometry_lod_shader_policy.py and tests/test_geometry_lod_numeric.py. This is
an explicit reference-evaluator profile, not an adoption of a fork's reduced
mantissa policy, a shader precision conformance claim or draw admission.

native_fragment7e3_depth.h adapts the raw-alpha-test / fixed-point alpha-clamp /
independent blend-term / per-write packing order from
src/xenia/gpu/spirv_shader_translator_rb.cc (Copyright 2022 Ben Vanik).
Local finite-input restrictions and explicit TRUNC20e4 policy do not claim
Xbox interpolation/rounding equivalence or authorize a live draw.

xenos_color_7e3.h's explicit Color7e3Rounding::XeniaShiftRound policy adapts
PreClampedFloat32To7e3 / UnclampedFloat32To7e3 from the same file. The finite-input
clamp, denormal bit shift, rounding bias and low ten-bit extraction follow that
algorithm, including lost sticky bits. The default stricter agreement policy
remains separate; neither policy is a measured Xbox hardware oracle.
